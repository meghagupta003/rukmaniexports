Trust in gemstones is built on two things working together: independent laboratory certification, and an expert eye that has spent decades learning what a certificate alone can't always convey. Neither replaces the other. Both matter.

How Certification Works

What GIA and GRS Certification Actually Tells You

Every gemstone we offer is independently certified by GIA, GRS, or another recognized gemological laboratory. A certificate confirms origin (where determinable), treatment status, and the specific characteristics that determine a stone's rarity and value — verified by a party with no stake in the sale.
