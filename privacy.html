Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to serve you well, and we treat it with the same discretion we'd want for our own family's information.

What We Collect

  • Contact details you provide directly — name, email, and message — when you use our inquiry form.
  • Order and shipping information — name, delivery address, and order details — when you make a purchase.
  • Payment information is collected and processed entirely by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number or payment credentials ourselves.

How We Use It

Solely to fulfill your order, respond to your inquiry, and communicate with you about a purchase or question. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes, ever.

Who We Share It With

  • Our payment provider — to process payments securely.
  • Our shipping carrier — solely to deliver your order.
  • We do not use these services to sell you anything beyond what you've already purchased or inquired about.

Cookies

Our site uses only the minimal, functional cookies required for the site to work correctly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time, by contacting us directly through our Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to us directly through the Contact page — we read and respond to these personally.
