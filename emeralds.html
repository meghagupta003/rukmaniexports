What Makes a Fine Natural Emerald

Emerald is valued first for its color — a specific, saturated green few other gemstones achieve. Nearly all fine emeralds contain natural inclusions, often called jardin (French for "garden"), and most undergo a widely accepted minor oil treatment to improve clarity.

None of this diminishes a stone's quality — what matters is transparency about it. Every emerald we offer states its treatment plainly, including oil treatment where present.

Our Approach

Honesty About Treatment, Every Time

Honesty about treatment is where our Honesty & Integrity value shows up most concretely in this category — emeralds are treated more often than not, and we say so, every time.
