Cat's Eye

A specialty that rewards a trained eye more than almost any other stone we trade.

What Makes a Fine Cat's Eye

Cat's eye chrysoberyl displays chatoyancy — a sharp, single band of reflected light across the stone's surface, caused by fine, parallel needle-like inclusions. The finest examples show a tight, well-centered "eye" that opens and closes cleanly as the stone is turned in light.

Judging this quality well is a genuinely specialized skill, and part of why this category sits within our disciplined, ranked specialization rather than a broad, unfocused catalog.
