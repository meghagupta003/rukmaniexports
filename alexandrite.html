Alexandrite

The rarest stone in our specialization — and one of the rarest in the gem trade entirely.

What Makes a Fine Natural Alexandrite

Alexandrite is known for a genuine color-change effect: green to bluish-green in daylight, red to purplish-red under incandescent light. Fine natural alexandrite showing a strong, clean color shift is exceptionally rare.

A specific, verifiable color-change reaction, confirmed by certification, is the single most important thing to look for before acquiring one.
