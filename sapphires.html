What Makes a Fine Natural Sapphire

Sapphire is corundum in every color except red (which is classified as ruby), though blue remains the most sought-after by collectors. As with rubies, treatment history matters enormously — a natural, unheated sapphire of fine color commands a significant premium over a heat-treated stone of similar visual appearance.

Origin plays a meaningful role too: Kashmir, Burmese, and Ceylon sapphires each carry distinct recognition among serious collectors. Every sapphire we offer states origin, treatment status, and certifying lab plainly.
