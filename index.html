Our Heritage

Every Gemstone Has A Story, So Does Ours.

In 1971, our family's journey with gemstones began in Jaipur's local markets — trading, evaluating, earning trust one relationship at a time. Three generations later, that same eye for quality guides Rukmani Exports today, alongside something new our third generation brought into the family tradition: hands-on artisanal jewellery-making, practiced by our in-house karigars.
